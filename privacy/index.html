支援 Support私隱政策 Privacy

簡單講 · In short

我哋唔會收集你的個人資料。App 冇帳戶、冇廣告、冇分析追蹤。你的學習進度只儲存在你的手機上。唯一經網絡處理的是你在 App Store／Google Play 的付款，由 Apple／Google 及我哋的付款服務商 RevenueCat 處理。

We do not collect your personal data. The app has no accounts, no ads and no analytics tracking. Your study progress is stored only on your device. The only thing that goes over the network is your purchase, handled by Apple / Google and our payment provider RevenueCat.

1. 我哋儲存咩資料 · What is stored

以下資料只儲存在你的裝置上，我哋無法讀取：答題進度及成績、收藏題目、生字簿、模擬試紀錄、設定（例如是否顯示中文）。刪除 App 即會清除。

The following is kept only on your device and is never sent to us: answers and scores, bookmarked questions, word book, mock-exam history and settings such as whether Chinese is shown. Deleting the app deletes it.

2. 付款 · Purchases

完整版為一次性應用程式內購買，由 Apple App Store 或 Google Play 處理，我哋唔會見到你的付款卡資料。為咗確認購買及支援「恢復購買」，我哋使用 RevenueCat 服務；RevenueCat 會收到一個隨機產生的匿名裝置識別碼及 Apple／Google 的購買收據，唔包括你的姓名或電郵。詳見 RevenueCat 私隱政策。

The full version is a one-time in-app purchase processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play; we never see your card details. To verify purchases and support “Restore purchase” we use RevenueCat, which receives a randomly generated anonymous device identifier and the Apple / Google receipt, but not your name or email. See the RevenueCat privacy policy.

3. 朗讀功能 · Read-aloud

「聆聽 English」使用你手機系統內置的語音合成（Apple／Google 文字轉語音），題目文字在你的裝置上處理，我哋唔會收到。

“聆聽 English” uses your phone’s built-in text-to-speech (Apple / Google). The text is processed on your device and nothing is sent to us.

「自然人聲朗讀」會在你第一次播放某句時，從我哋嘅網站（GitHub Pages，由 GitHub 託管）下載該句嘅錄音檔，並儲存在你嘅手機離線使用。GitHub 會如一般網站訪問般記錄你嘅 IP 地址。錄音由 Google Cloud 文字轉語音預先生成；App 唔會將你嘅任何資料傳送畀 Google。你可以在「更多 → 聲音」關閉自然人聲，改用手機內置語音。

“Natural voice” read-aloud downloads a pre-recorded clip for a sentence the first time you play it, from our website (GitHub Pages, hosted by GitHub), and keeps it on your phone for offline use. GitHub sees your IP address as with any website visit. The clips were generated in advance with Google Cloud Text-to-Speech; the app sends none of your data to Google. You can switch natural voice off in More → Audio to use the phone’s built-in voice instead.

4. 聯絡我哋 · Contacting us

如你電郵畀我哋，我哋會保留你的電郵及內容以便回覆，之後唔會用作其他用途或轉交第三方。

If you email us, we keep your email address and message to reply, and do not use them for anything else or share them with third parties.

5. 第三方連結 · Third-party links

「相片來源」頁的連結會以瀏覽器開啟 Wikimedia Commons 網頁，該等網站有自己的私隱政策。

Links on the “Photo credits” page open Wikimedia Commons in your browser, which has its own privacy policy.

6. 兒童 · Children

本 App 唔會刻意向 13 歲以下兒童收集任何資料，實際上亦唔會收集任何人的個人資料。

The app does not knowingly collect data from children under 13, and in practice collects no personal data from anyone.

7. 你的權利 · Your rights

根據英國 GDPR 及《2018 年數據保護法》，你有權查閱、更正或刪除我哋持有的個人資料。由於我哋基本上唔持有你的資料，刪除 App 已足以清除所有本機資料；購買紀錄請聯絡 Apple／Google。有任何疑問可電郵我哋，亦可向英國資訊專員辦公室 (ICO) 投訴。

Under the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018 you may access, correct or erase personal data we hold. As we hold essentially none, deleting the app removes all local data; for purchase records contact Apple / Google. You may email us with any question and have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO).

8. 更改 · Changes

如政策有更改，我哋會在此頁更新生效日期。

If this policy changes we will update the effective date on this page.
