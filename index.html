關於這個 App · About the app

Life in the UK 港人版 幫你準備英國入籍／永居的 Life in the UK 考試。內容根據官方手冊《Life in the United Kingdom: A Guide for New Residents》（第三版）編寫，全部題目附中英對照、提示、小知識及英文朗讀。

A bilingual (English / Traditional Chinese) study app for the UK “Life in the UK” test, made for Hong Kongers. Based on the official handbook, 3rd edition, with hints, did-you-know facts and English read-aloud for every question.

免費下載，第一章及一次模擬試免費。一次過付款 £2.99 永久解鎖全部內容，無月費、無廣告。

Free to download; Chapter 1 and one mock exam are free. A one-time £2.99 purchase unlocks everything for life. No subscription, no ads.

常見問題 · FAQ

我換了手機，點樣恢復已購買的完整版？ How do I restore my purchase on a new phone?

用同一個 Apple ID／Google 帳戶登入，開啟 App → 更多 → 「恢復購買 Restore purchase」。

Sign in with the same Apple ID / Google account, then open More → “恢復購買 Restore purchase”.

學習進度會唔會同步到其他裝置？ Does my progress sync between devices?

唔會。所有進度、收藏及生字簿只儲存在你的手機上，刪除 App 會一併清除。

No. Progress, bookmarks and the word book are stored only on your device and are removed when you delete the app.

朗讀冇聲？ Read-aloud is silent?

請確認手機未開靜音，並在系統設定下載英式英文 (en-GB) 語音。

Check the phone is not on silent and that a British English (en-GB) voice is installed in the system settings.

發現題目或翻譯有錯？ Found a mistake in a question or translation?

請電郵話我哋知，註明章節及題號，我哋會核對官方手冊後更正。

Please email us with the chapter and question number; we check against the official handbook and fix it.
